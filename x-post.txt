In a P2P crypto trade, the seller releases coins after looking at a payment alert. Alerts are easy to fake.

I built Provia: an escrow agent that releases only when the payment is proven, and the contract checks the proof on @ZKVProtocol.

Try to cheat it: https://provia-ten.vercel.app
#AgentGames

2/
How a release works:
• seller locks USDC in an escrow on Base Sepolia
• buyer pays and tells the agent "I've paid, ref X"
• zkTLS proves what the payment processor's own API returned
• a Groth16 circuit proves the trade rules hold
#AgentGames

3/
The proof goes to zkVerify mainnet for finality, and to zkVerify testnet where it is aggregated to Base Sepolia.

The escrow rebuilds the leaf from its own trade data and calls verifyProofAggregation. No attestation, no release. The agent can't talk its way past it.
#AgentGames

4/
Four attacks, all refused live with typed codes:
• forged screenshot → NO_PAYMENT_AT_PSP
• underpayment → AMOUNT_SHORT
• someone else's payment → TRADE_MISMATCH
• reused payment → REF_REUSED
Every refusal is a public receipt.
#AgentGames
